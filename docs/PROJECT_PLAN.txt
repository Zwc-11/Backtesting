Validation gate
Rolling walk-forward with doubled costs is the right core, and it is not enough alone: it does not stop you from trying many ideas until one passes. A result counts only if it clears all ten rules.
1. Register first. The hypothesis, parameter grid and data range go into the trial registry before any data is read. Every run is logged, including failures.
2. Vault. The most recent 20% of history is locked and opened once per hypothesis family.
3. Purged, embargoed walk-forward. Parameters are chosen on the training window only; the embargo is at least the longest holding period. Hindsight does this today.
4. Costs at 1x and 2x. Commission, spread and slippage come from costs.yaml per asset class. The result must stay positive at 2x.
5. Execution realism. A signal at bar close fills no earlier than the next bar's open; stops are checked first inside a bar; no fills on bars with no trades.
6. Selection-adjusted statistics. Report the stitched out-of-sample t-statistic by day, the Deflated Sharpe Ratio and the Probability of Backtest Overfitting, using the registry's trial count.
7. No single-trade dependence. The result must stay positive with the best 5% of trades removed, and no single fold may supply more than half the profit.
8. Leakage tests in CI. Truncating future bars must not change any feature; delaying the signal one bar must shrink the edge, not flip or grow it.
9. Independent audit. A second reviewer that did not write the code reads it for look-ahead before the verdict is accepted.
10. Cross-engine rerun. Anything that passes is rerun in NautilusTrader; differences are explained in RECONCILIATION.md.

An edge moves through four states, stored in one DuckDB table with source, target, type, horizon, lag, strength, t-statistic, adjusted p-value, window and status.
1. Candidate: estimated on a rolling window.
2. Significant: survives false-discovery control across every pair and lag tested, not a per-pair threshold.
3. Stable: same sign and at least half the strength in the next window.
4. Tradable: a strategy built on it has passed the validation gate.
The dashboard shows states 2 and 3 as the descriptive map and highlights state 4. With about 150 instruments there are roughly 22,000 ordered pairs per horizon, so false-discovery control decides whether the map means anything.

Limits and open decisions
Five limits are built into the free route and should be read before the first result.
• CFD proxies are not futures. Dukascopy quotes carry no exchange volume, a broker's spread and their own trading hours. Use them to find candidates, then confirm on CME bars.
• Non-US stocks and futures contracts start today. Their 1-minute history is whatever the recorder has saved.
• Yahoo is unofficial. It can change or block without notice, so the recorder needs a health alert and, where one exists, a second source.
• Survivorship. Free equity sources omit delisted names; results on small caps will look better than they were.
• A map shows dependence, not profit. The system's first job is to reject ideas cheaply. Nothing here predicts a return, and none of it is investment advice.
