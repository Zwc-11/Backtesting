      Runs

      Does the signal add information?

      Each model with the proposed signal is compared out of sample with the same model on the ordinary controls only. Positive values favour the signal; an interval that spans zero is not evidence.

      Walk-forward folds

      Do the entries predict direction?

      Price move after each signal in basis points, before any cost and regardless of the exit, next to entries at random times on the same coins and times of day. A real edge shows up here first; it has to exceed the round-trip cost (about 13 to 20 bp on perpetuals) to pay.

      Results by market regime

      The same trades split by what the market (the benchmark) was doing when each decision was made: mean net result per trade in basis points, with the number of trades.
